Microcycle Block // Session A
Linear & Eccentric Load Focus
Equipment: Kettlebell, Dumbbell, Pull-Up Bar
Target Pace: Deterministic (Rest as instructed)
Engine Integration: StrongFirst Q&D
Block 1: Neurological Switch (RPR Warm-Up)
[CNS & Slings] RPR Full Wake-Up Drill
Warm-Up
🎯
Target Zone & WHY: Psoas, Glutes & Cervical Spine. Flips neurological switches to remove central compensation patterns before loading.
📐
Exact Setup Cues: Vigorously rub jawline/ribs for glutes, and belly button radius for psoas. Perform 30s saccadic eye flicks between thumbs.
⏱️
Timer & Dose: 13 Minutes total continuous flow.
✅
Success Metric: 360° diaphragmatic expansion felt; posture feels upright and light.
[Lower Leg & Achilles] 3-Way Ankle Pogos
Warm-Up
🎯
Target Zone & WHY: Calves & Achilles Tendon. Primes the lower leg stiffness and elastic recoil for the upcoming session.
📐
Exact Setup Cues: Stiff knees, bounce off the balls of the feet. Minimal ground contact time.
⏱️
Timer & Dose: 15 Seconds Total: 5s neutral foot, 5s toes pointed in, 5s toes pointed out.
✅
Success Metric: Fast, springy contacts with no heel strike.
Block 2: Structural Isometrics (Armor Holds)
[Pecs & Anterior Core] Isometric Push-Up
Iso Hold
🎯
Target Zone & WHY: Pectorals, shoulders & core. Develops absolute upper-body structural stiffness and positional endurance.
📐
Exact Setup Cues: Hover chest 2 inches off the floor. Actively "pull" your hands together into the floor to maximize pec engagement.
⏱️
Timer & Dose: Hold 60 Seconds @ 40% effort → Rest 60s → 1 Set.
✅
Success Metric: Rigid plank maintained; no hip sagging or shoulder shrugging.
[Quads & Patellar] Bodyweight Isometric Split Squat
Iso Hold
🎯
Target Zone & WHY: Patellar tendon & Quad muscle belly. Rebuilds collagen density and flushes joint inflammation.
📐
Exact Setup Cues: Back knee suspended 2 inches off floor. Torso 100% vertical. Weight split 50/50. Smooth 3s force ramp up.
⏱️
Timer & Dose: Hold 60 Seconds per leg @ 40% effort → Rest 60s → 1 Set per side.
✅
Success Metric: Stable posture, no early shake-out, zero sharp joint pain.
[Ankles & Achilles] Spring Ankle Hold - Level 1
Iso Hold
🎯
Target Zone & WHY: Achilles tendon & Plantar fascia. Builds foot stiffness for acceleration and landing absorption.
📐
Exact Setup Cues: Barefoot. Drive ball of foot into ground, raise heel 1 inch. Knee locked straight.
⏱️
Timer & Dose: Hold 60 Seconds per side → Rest 60s → 1 Set.
✅
Success Metric: Zero ankle collapse; solid foot arch engagement.
Block 3: Ballistics & PAP (Superset x 4 Sets)
1A. [Lower Chain] 6" Altitude Depth Drop
Ballistic
🎯
Target Zone & WHY: Fast-twitch motor units (ACTN3). Teaches elastic force absorption under zero fatigue.
📐
Exact Setup Cues: Step off 6" box. Land in athletic stance with "eggshell heels" (silent impact, freeze 2s).
⏱️
Timer & Dose: 10 Reps → Rest 60s before 1B.
✅
Success Metric: Silent landing, zero knee caving (valgus), instant freeze.
1B. [Cervical Spine] Isometric Neck Exercise (Front & Back)
Active Filler
🎯
Target Zone & WHY: Cervical flexors & extensors. Dissipates kinetic shock to protect against concussion.
📐
Exact Setup Cues: Palms against forehead/behind head. Press head into hands at 30% effort with neutral spine.
⏱️
Timer & Dose: 30 Seconds hold → Rest 60s → Repeat Superset for 4 Sets.
✅
Success Metric: Rigid neck posture; zero chin protrusion.
Block 4: Strength Concentrics & Active Fillers (3 Sets)
2A. [Upper Pull] Bodyweight Pull-Ups
Concentric
🎯
Target Zone & WHY: Lats & Scapular retractors. Builds absolute upper body pulling power and shoulder armor.
📐
Exact Setup Cues: Full dead hang start. Drive elbows down to ribs, chest to bar. No swinging or kicking.
⏱️
Timer & Dose: 2 to 3 Reps (Dice Roll adjusted) → Rest until breathing normal.
✅
Success Metric: Crisp chin over bar; smooth controlled descent.
2B. [Shin & Knee] Bodyweight Deficit Tibialis Raise
Active Filler
🎯
Target Zone & WHY: Tibialis Anterior. Bulletproofs the front of the shin to decelerate impact and protect knees.
📐
Exact Setup Cues: Heels on step edge. Lower toes into deep stretch, flex toes straight up to ceiling.
⏱️
Timer & Dose: 20 Reps → Rest 60s → Repeat Superset for 3 Sets.
✅
Success Metric: Full ankle range of motion; intense tibialis contraction.
Block 5: StrongFirst Engine (Alactic Conditioning)
⚡ Quick & The Dead (Q&D) Protocol
Launch Q&D Timer ➔
🎯
Biological Objective: Alactic Explosive Power (ATP-CP System). Zero lactic acid accumulation, preserving motor unit velocity.
📐
Movement Selection: Single-Arm Kettlebell Snatch OR Power Swings (Heavy Bell, RPE 7-8).
⏱️
Dose & Pacing: 10 Seconds Work (3-5 Max Intent Reps) → 50 Seconds Active Nasal Recovery. Complete 2 to 4 Series of 5 Bursts (10-20 mins total).
✅
Stop Rule: The exact second bell speed drops or nasal breathing breaks, terminate the session immediately.
Block 6: Vagal Reset (Parasympathetic Shift)
[Nervous System] Decompression Hang & 1:4:2 Breathing
Reset
🎯
Target Zone & WHY: Spinal decompression & Vagus nerve. Down-regulates sympathetic adrenaline back to recovery state.
📐
Exact Setup Cues: One-handed passive bar hang (feet touched on floor for support) for 1 min per arm. Follow with 1:4:2 nasal breathing.
⏱️
Timer & Dose: 3 Minutes total breathwork (Inhale 4s → Hold 16s → Exhale 8s).
✅
Success Metric: Heart rate normalized; complete parasympathetic shift before leaving.

Autonomic Diagnostics
Morning CO2 "Dice Roll" Guidelines

Use your morning exhale time (in seconds) compared to your baseline average to autoregulate today's concentric volume.

Master CO2 Autoregulation Scale
🔴 RED DAY (15+ Seconds Below Baseline)
CNS Burnout
Action: NO HEAVY LIFTING. Skip main workout. Execute 100% RPR resets, light nasal walks, and 3 minutes of 1:4:2 vagal breathing.
🟡 BELOW AVERAGE CO2
Under-Recovered
Action: EXECUTE 1 SET ONLY. You are surviving today. Stimulate tissue, cap volume early, and exit the gym.
🟢 AVERAGE CO2
Adapted & Ready
Action: EXECUTE 2 SETS. Standard baseline training volume. Leave 2-3 reps in the tank (RPE 7-8).
⚡ ABOVE AVERAGE CO2
Fully Primed
Action: EXECUTE 3 SETS. Your central nervous system is fully primed. Push maximum athletic intent and velocity.
